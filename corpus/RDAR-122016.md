# rdar-122016: Single latency outlier on one unit

## Description
One measurement on one unit exceeded the latency limit at room temperature. No
firmware or hardware change was associated with the build.

## Root cause and resolution
The fixture probe contact was intermittent. A retest passed. Closed as test
infrastructure flakiness, not a product regression.
