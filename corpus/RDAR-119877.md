# rdar-119877: Input lag increase of about 5 ms after battery optimisation

## Description
Touch response felt sluggish on build 18. Latency rose by roughly 5 ms at every
temperature and on every unit, crossing the 12 ms limit.

## Root cause and resolution
The panel refresh frequency had been halved to save power. Doubling it back resolved
the lag.
