# rdar-121530: Elevated baseline noise on a subset of test units

## Description
Two units showed a noise floor near 47 uV while the others stayed around 22 uV. The
extra noise appeared at every temperature, but only on those two units.

## Root cause and resolution
Both units had parts from an alternate supplier lot. They were returned to the
original lot, and incoming inspection was added for the new supplier.
