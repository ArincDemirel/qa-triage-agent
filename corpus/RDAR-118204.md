# rdar-118204: Calibration offset out of spec in the cold after smoothing change

## Description
On build 12 the calibration offset exceeded the 1.0 mV limit at -20C on every unit.
Room temperature and hot were unaffected. The build had increased smoothing in the
touch filter to make scrolling feel more fluid.

## Root cause and resolution
With more smoothing the filter settles slowly at low temperature, so the offset
estimate lags. Restoring the previous smoothing setting brought the offset back to
about 0.35 mV.
